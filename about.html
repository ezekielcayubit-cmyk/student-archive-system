Welcome

Student Archive Management System

A web-based archive platform designed for Young Achievers' School of Caloocan, Inc. to organize, store, and manage students' research papers in one secure and accessible location.

What the System Can Do

Archive Research

Store approved research papers in an organized digital archive.

Quick Search

Find research papers instantly using keywords or titles.

View & Download

Open and download research documents anytime.

Digital Preservation

Keep valuable research outputs organized for future reference.

Why This System?

Organized digital archive
Faster document retrieval
Easy file management
Supports teachers and students
Preserves research records
